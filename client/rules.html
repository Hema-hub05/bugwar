📜 BugWar Rules

Please read all the rules before starting the competition.

Exactly 2 participants per team.
Each team can sign in only once.
The competition consists of 3 rounds.
Each round contains 10 questions.
Each round lasts 15 minutes.
Score at least 70% in Round 1 to unlock Round 2.
The quiz must remain in full-screen mode.
Leaving full-screen or switching tabs is monitored.
Refreshing the page during the quiz is not allowed.
Organizer's decision will be final.
